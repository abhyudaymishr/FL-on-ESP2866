THE LEARNING LOOP

Physics first. Learning at the edge.

Each ESP8266 observes temperature, estimates diffusion using its four-neighbor stencil, and trains a quadratic residual with Adam. The elected board combines sample-weighted model updates.

01Observe

Local temperature and fresh neighbor signals.

02Predict & learn

Diffusion plus standardized quadratic correction.

03Share & aggregate

Boot-aware model updates and sample-weighted FedAvg.

The cloud visualizes observations. It does not train, command, or flash the boards.

READ THE METRICS CORRECTLY

Evidence before conclusions.

Local and contributor losses describe training samples. They cannot establish generalization, physical accuracy, or improvement over a baseline.

Use the repository's frozen holdout benchmark to compare persistence, physics-only, local, centralized, and federated models.

python3 experiments/run_benchmark.py

A node's model expires after 15 seconds at capture. Heartbeats do not refresh training data.
